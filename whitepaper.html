CAGlorie Lite Whitepaper

How the game works, what each NFT does, and where the weekly prize pool comes from.

RONIN MAINNET · SEPTEMBER 2026

🍽️ What is CAGlorie

CAGlorie is a tap-to-feed game on Ronin: you feed CAG, every meal adds calories, and each week the players who ate enough share a $CAG prize pool.

You can play, qualify and win for free with the Snack. NFTs and $CAG make you faster, they are not a ticket in.

📅 How a week works

Each week runs Monday 00:00 UTC to the next Monday 00:00 UTC. Reach 2,000 calories in a week and you qualify for that week's pool.

  • Calories reset to zero when a new week starts.
  • After a week closes, qualified players have one week to claim. The claim button appears in the game on its own.
  • Whatever is left unclaimed once that week is over is rolled into an upcoming weekly pool, so it goes back to the players.

🔑 CAG NFT: the access key

Holding a CAG NFT unlocks the Meal and the Big Order. It is never used up: you keep it and it keeps working every week. One is enough.

A holder who never spends $CAG earns roughly 9× the calories of a Snack-only player, because the Meal and the Big Order add up fast.

🥤 Drinks NFT: the consumable

Drinks are NFTs you feed to CAG. Each one is a single serving: drink it and it's gone. The first one is the Energy Drink, worth 1,400–1,800 calories, every 6 h.

  • How to drink: press the button on the game page. It sends one NFT from your wallet to the game in a single transaction, no approval needed.
  • Best value in the game: priced close to a Chocolate but worth more calories on average, which makes it the most efficient purchase on the menu.
  • More drinks are coming. Each new drink gets its own calories and cooldown, and they stack with each other.

Get one on Ronin Market ↗

💰 The prize pool

Every week's pool is paid in $CAG and starts at 1,000,000 $CAG. What players spend in the game goes back into it.

  • $CAG spent returns to the pool. Every $CAG paid for a Supersize or a Chocolate is added back to the weekly prize pool.
  • 50% of Drinks sales return to the pool. Half of the RON from Drinks NFT sales is converted to $CAG and added to the weekly prize pool.
  • The split is proportional. Your share = pool × your calories ÷ total calories of all qualified players. Your leaderboard rank does not change what you get, only your calories do.
Example. If the pool is 1,000,000 $CAG and qualified players ate 2,000,000 calories in total, each calorie is worth 0.5 $CAG. A player with 20,000 calories claims 10,000 $CAG.
